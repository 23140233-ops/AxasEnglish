📚 Guía Rápida: Will vs. Going to
1. Be going to + infinitivo
  • Planes ya decididos: "I'm going to specialize in data science."
  • Evidencia presente: "With these grades, I'm going to thrive."
2. Will + infinitivo
  • Promesas y compromisos: "I will work hard every day."
  • Decisiones espontáneas: "That's a great point—I'll consider it."
  • Predicciones / Opiniones: "I believe AI will transform healthcare."
